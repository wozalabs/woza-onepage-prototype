# Woza Labs — one-page prototype

Concept prototype for the Woza Labs one-page site.

**Live:** https://wozalabs.github.io/woza-onepage-prototype/

- `index.html`: the page. It has no dependencies or build step.
- `nave.html`: the nave variant. A walk through a meadow to a loggia door; through it, the site6 content as eight chapters on a 2D page, with the chapter nav at the foot (a scrolling strip on phones). Signals and Team switch the page to its light or dark counterpart with a colour fade. Its Version menu shows only Nave.
- `coral.html`: the reef variant. A ray-marched coral reef you move through by scrolling; Export renders stills (deck, A4, square) of the current view or a single coral, on the canvas or transparent.
- `coast.html`: the coast variant. The shoreline of Bizkaia (OpenStreetMap) measured with dividers; scroll halves the ruler and descends through four scales, from the whole coast to millimetres. A lens refines the measurement under the pointer; Export renders stills with or without the readout.
- `shore.html`: the shore variant. The full content read top to bottom on an uneven block grid; behind it the coast of Bizkaia, its land drawn by the current ruler, refined one step per section as the view drifts into the ría at Bilbao. The four platforms are plates at four scales; hovering one refines it.
- `graft.html`: the graft variant. A grower never starts from seed: the tree at the left is the menu (a branch per deployment, a fruit per venture, a root per engine, a seedling per piece of research), redrawn from a new seed on every load. Each deployment is a plate drawn twice, joined at the graft union: above the seam as it looks, below it as Woza models it; the seam rises as you scroll and follows the pointer. The Index (top right, or the / key) lists every subject A to Z.
- `plain.html`: the plain variant. The full text in one readable column, with the list of sections beside it (a Contents bar on phones). Neue Montreal Regular only, hierarchy from size and space, no labels above headings and no rules. Follows the system light or dark setting.
- `portico.html`: the portico variant. A sheet of drawings you move along sideways: scrolling slides the content to the left, and the ruler at the foot shows the whole sheet, its chapters and where you are (press or drag it to go anywhere). The content sits in gapless cells of a 12-column grid, spaced only by their own padding, with two empty columns before each new chapter; each cell is ruled in as it arrives. Every call to action is a whole cell carrying one of three landscape photographs (`media/cta-*.webp`): at rest it is printed in coloured dots on the page (the same dot size and Bayer threshold as the buildings' shade; the darker the photograph, the closer the dots, each dot in the photograph's own colour, a little deeper), and on hover the original photograph comes up (no pixel lighter than #F2F0EB), with the words on tabs of page colour that turn to ink. The two empty columns before each chapter carry a soft band of the same dots, thinning out towards the rules; anything else that opens somewhere gets a light Bayer dither under the pointer, the same dither as the drawings' shade. Buildings are drawn whole inside their cells, never cropped. The ruler's window is a plain tint. The ventures (Predicterra, Hectareo, AboveForest) stay plain until pointed at, when each shows its own landscape (`media/v-*.webp`). The sheet ends on a cell with the asterisk of the mark in #F2F0EB over a photograph, dotted at rest and in full on hover. Partners get a page of their own, one logo per cell. Client and partner logos are inline SVG (vectorised from the logo files on the current site; Microsoft's squares from its official mark). Its colours are fixed (no random palette): #434343 at a few opacities on #F2F0EB, which is the brightest value on the page. Each chapter has a Renaissance building (Tempietto, Innocenti loggia, Todi, Brunelleschi's dome, La Rotonda and others) drawn from the ground up as its cell comes in: outlines only, filled with the page colour, shade and shadow as an ordered dither. Left alone, each building turns slowly; drag one, or use the arrow keys, to turn it by hand; Plan, Elevation and Isometric set the view. Neue Montreal throughout, one word per statement in Pinyon Script, every line set slightly tight. Phones get the same cells stacked down the page.
- `atrium.html`: the atrium variant. Portico with the room cleared, for easier reading: one thing per screen. Each chapter opens on a single plate (a statement beside one drawing), and what follows comes as short lists, one item to a row (deployments, ventures, research, signals), or a few large cells (engines, team, partners). Larger type, lighter rules, a simpler header and foot, and the drawing controls appear only when you point at or focus a drawing. Same sideways sheet, 12-column gapless cells, two empty columns before each chapter, fixed #434343 on #F2F0EB, Neue Montreal with Pinyon Script, buildings drawn whole and turning slowly when left alone, the same photographs behind each call to action (coloured dots at rest, the photograph on hover), the venture photographs on hover, dotted bands between chapters, and the asterisk cell at the end.
- `media/cta-meadow.webp`, `media/cta-slope.webp`, `media/cta-vista.webp`: the call-to-action photographs used by Portico and Atrium (the slope also sits behind the asterisk).
- `media/v-predicterra.webp`, `media/v-hectareo.webp`, `media/v-aboveforest.webp`: the venture photographs shown on hover.
- `fonts/PinyonScript-Regular.woff2`: Pinyon Script (latin subset) under the SIL Open Font License, see `fonts/PinyonScript-OFL.txt`.
- `fonts/NeueMontreal-Regular.woff2`: the web font, used under Woza Labs' commercial licence. Don't reuse it outside Woza projects.

Every load picks a random colour palette, checked against WCAG AAA before the first paint.
